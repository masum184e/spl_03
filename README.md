<summary>What is review paper? Should you include it in your literature review?</summary>

A review paper is a type of academic article that summarizes, analyzes, and synthesizes existing research on a specific topic instead of presenting new experimental results. Think of it as a “big picture” paper—it gathers many studies and explains trends, debates, gaps, and conclusions in the field.

**What a review paper does**

- Collects results from many previous studies
- Compares and contrasts findings
- Identifies patterns, disagreements, or gaps
- Suggests future research directions

**You should not include it in your literature review.**

- Your literature review should mainly include primary research articles (original studies)
- Review papers are secondary sources, so overusing them can make your work less strong academically

Start with review papers to build understanding, use their references to find key original studies
