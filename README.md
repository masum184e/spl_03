### Thesis Proposal

<details> <summary>What Actually Happens in a Thesis Proposal?</summary>

We are instructed to submit our thesis proposal document, which will be reviewed and given feedback by the committee.

The proposal document must follow the provided template and should be prepared after discussing and reviewing the research topic and proposal with our supervisor.

Before submission, the proposal will go through Turnitin to check for plagiarism and AI generated content, as required by the department.

After the committee reviews the proposal, there are two possible outcomes:

- If the proposal is approved, we are good to proceed with the thesis.
- If the committee requires changes, we need to make the required revisions according to the committee's feedback and resubmit the revised proposal.

No presentation, or one to one viva happend here. Just submitted the documents in the office.

### Thesis Mid

<details>

<summary>What documents and progress do we need to prepare for the Mid term Defence?</summary>

**Thesis Report** *[Soft Copy]*

The thesis report should mainly include the Introduction section with the Motivation, Problem Statement, Research Question, and Contribution, along with a complete Literature Review.

**1-Page Summary** *[Hard Copy + Supervisor's Signature]*

The summary should include the Project Title, Research Question, Implementation Pipeline, and a brief meeting/progress summary with dates for each meeting held with the supervisor from proposal submission to the mid-term.

**1-Page Literature Summary** *[Soft Copy]*

The literature summary should contain at least 10 related works, covering their pipelines, methods, pros and cons, results, and relevant comments or observations.

**Code Base**

The code base should demonstrate the implementation progress according to the proposed project pipeline. **If a dataset is used**, its details and references to previous studies that used the dataset should also be provided.

<details>

<summary>What are the requirements and instructions for the Defence Mid?</summary>

The Mid term Defence Board list has been provided, and each student has to contact their respective teacher to know their defence schedule. The schedule will be decided by the respective board.

Each student must bring a laptop to show their project progress and necessary documents.

Students who do not have the supervisor's signature on the hard copy of the summary sheet will not be allowed to enter the board and will receive a penalty in the midterm.

No presentation happened here, but one to one viva conduct with respective teacher(ovbiously not your supervisor).
